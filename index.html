The deal, in plain terms

No fine print games. Every dollar your music earns splits three ways, and the biggest share is yours.

60%You, the artist

The split on net revenue from your distributed music.

Your masters stay yours

This is strictly distribution. Ice Cold takes no ownership of your recordings and no creative control over your music. Your record stays your record.

Your music stays up forever

Once your music is distributed through Ice Cold, it stays live on the platforms. There is no yearly fee holding your catalog hostage and no takedown when a subscription lapses.

Your publishing gets registered

Ice Cold does not administer your publishing, but your publishing is registered on every work we distribute, so the writing side of your music is on the record from day one.

Digital only

Distribution is digital, across the full retail and streaming network. There are no physical copies in this deal.

No advance

There is no advance in this deal. What you earn is your 60 percent of what your music makes, and it starts with the strength of the record itself.

No marketing budget

There is no marketing budget attached. Ice Cold puts your music everywhere it can be heard. The push behind it is yours to build, and you keep the masters while you build it.

About the label

Ice Cold Recordings was born in Brooklyn in late 2010, founded by Roger “Divine” Davy alongside DJ Nuclear and Sergio. The label's first release was Met Bronson, and the name has been moving ever since.

Divine came up in East Flatbush, in the heart of Little Caribbean, and learned the music business from the road. He started as a roadie in the nineties and rose to tour manager, working with some of the biggest names in hip hop and R&B before stepping into education. The road taught him what no classroom teaches: how records really move, market to market, stage to stage.

Now Ice Cold is signing again, with a distribution deal through On Top Records and Virgin Music Group, and one mission. Flood the market with real records from independent artists who keep what is theirs.
